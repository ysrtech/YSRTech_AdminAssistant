The **AI Assistant** button in the admin header opens a chat beside the page. Ask in plain words:

- "How many orders shipped yesterday?"
- "Top 20 customers this year"
- "What's wrong with order 100000123?" or, on an order's page, "what's wrong with this order?"
- "Sales this week compared with last week"
- "Which products are low on stock?"
- "How did the last campaign do?" / "Did jane@example.com open our emails?"
- "How do I create a coupon code?" (answers from this wiki)
- "Is cron running?" / "Any errors in the logs today?"

It **only reads**. It cannot change, refund, send or delete anything; it explains how and where.

## Good to know

- It knows the page you are on: an order, customer, product or campaign. The chip above the question box shows it; click × to ask without it.
- It shows each look-up as it makes it. Your role decides what it may look up.
- Chats are kept for you only, with **New chat** (+) and earlier chats (clock icon). Ctrl/⌘ K opens it.
- Answers can be wrong. Check before acting on anything important.

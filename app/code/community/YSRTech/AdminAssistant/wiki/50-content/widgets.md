Widgets put ready-made content (product rows, category tiles, static blocks, links) into pages.

## Inside one page or block

In the editor, click **Insert Widget**, choose the **Widget Type**, fill its options, **Insert Widget**. This adds a `{{widget …}}` line to the content.

## On many pages at once

1. [CMS > Widgets](admin:widget_instance) > **Add New Widget Instance**.
2. **Type** and **Design Package/Theme** (the storefront theme), **Continue**.
3. **Widget Instance Title**, **Assign to Store Views**, **Sort Order**.
4. **Layout Updates** > **Add Layout Update**: which pages (e.g. **All Pages**, **Anchor Categories**, a specific page) and the **Block Reference** (where on the page).
5. **Widget Options** tab: the widget's own settings.
6. **Save**.

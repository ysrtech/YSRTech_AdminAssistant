---
title: Find an order
category: Orders
sort: 105
---
1. Go to [Sales > Orders](admin:sales_order).
2. Type in the filter boxes under the column headings: **Order #**, **Bill-to Name**, **Ship-to Name**, a date range in **Purchased On**, a **Status**. Press **Search**.
3. Click the row to open the order.

To find all orders of one customer, open the customer under [Customers > Manage Customers](admin:customer) and use the **Orders** tab. Guest orders only appear there if they were linked to the account (see [[guest-to-customer]]).

> [!TIP]
> The AI Assistant finds orders from a description: "orders from jane@example.com this month", "pending orders older than 3 days", "orders with SKU ABC-1".

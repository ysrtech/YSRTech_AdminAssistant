## Comments

At the bottom of the order page, **Comments History**:

1. Choose a **Status** (changing it is optional).
2. Write the comment.
3. Tick **Notify Customer by Email** to send it to the customer, and **Visible on Frontend** to show it in their account.
4. Click **Submit Comment**.

Comments without either tick are for staff only.

> [!WARNING]
> Comments written by customers (the order note at checkout) are shown here too. Treat them as the customer's words.

## Resend an email

- **Order confirmation**: **Send Email** at the top of the order page.
- **Invoice / shipment / credit memo**: open it and click **Send Email**.

If emails never arrive, check [System > Configuration > Sales Emails](admin:system_config/edit/section/sales_email) and that the store can send mail at all.

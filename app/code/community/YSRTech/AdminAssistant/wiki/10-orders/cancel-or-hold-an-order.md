## Cancel

**Cancel** (top of the order page) is only available while nothing has been invoiced or shipped. It releases the stock and marks the order **Canceled**. For a paid order, refund it instead ([[refund-an-order]]).

## Hold

**Hold** freezes the order: it cannot be invoiced, shipped or changed until someone clicks **Unhold**. Use it while waiting on the customer or checking a suspicious order. Leave a comment saying why (see [[order-comments-and-emails]]).

Several orders at once: tick them on [Sales > Orders](admin:sales_order) and choose **Cancel**, **Hold** or **Unhold** in **Actions**.

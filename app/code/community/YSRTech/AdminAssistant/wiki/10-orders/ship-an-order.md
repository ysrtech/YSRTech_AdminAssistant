---
title: Ship an order and add tracking
category: Orders
sort: 120
---
1. Open the order from [Sales > Orders](admin:sales_order).
2. Click **Ship** at the top.
3. Under **Add Tracking Number**, click **Add Tracking Number**, choose the **Carrier**, type a **Title** (shown to the customer) and the **Number**.
4. Check **Qty to Ship** for each item. Lower it for a partial shipment; ship the rest later the same way.
5. Tick **Email Copy of Shipment** to send the customer the shipping email with the tracking number.
6. Click **Submit Shipment**.

When the order is invoiced and everything is shipped, it becomes **Complete**.

## Add tracking later

Open the shipment from the order's **Shipments** tab (or [Sales > Shipments](admin:sales_shipment)), add the number under **Shipping and Tracking Information**, and click **Send Tracking Information** to email it.

## Packing slips

**Print** on a shipment gives a packing slip. On the orders grid, tick orders and choose **Print Packingslips** in **Actions**.

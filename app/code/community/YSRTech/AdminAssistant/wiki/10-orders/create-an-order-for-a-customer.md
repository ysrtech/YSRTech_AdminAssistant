---
title: Create an order for a customer
category: Orders
sort: 160
---
For phone or in-person orders.

1. Go to [Sales > Orders](admin:sales_order) and click **Create New Order**.
2. Pick the customer from the grid, or **Create New Customer**.
3. Choose the store if asked.
4. **Add Products**: search, tick the products, set quantities, **Add Selected Product(s) to Order**.
5. Check the billing and shipping addresses.
6. Under **Shipping Method**, click **Get shipping methods and rates** and choose one.
7. Choose the **Payment Method**.
8. Add a comment if needed; untick **Email Order Confirmation** if the customer should not get the email.
9. Click **Submit Order**.

A coupon can be applied under **Apply Coupon Code**, and a price changed per item with **Custom Price**.

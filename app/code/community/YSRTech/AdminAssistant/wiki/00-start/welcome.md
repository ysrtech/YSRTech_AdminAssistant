This wiki explains how things are done in this store: OpenMage itself, and every YSRTech module installed here. Everyone with the **Wiki** permission can read it; people whose role allows it can write and edit pages.

The **AI Assistant** reads these pages too. When someone asks it "how do I…", it answers from the wiki first, so a page written here is also what the assistant tells people.

## Finding things

- **Search** with the box at the top of the list on the left. It looks in titles and in the text of every page.
- **Browse** by category in the list on the left, or from the cards on the wiki's home page.
- **Ask the AI Assistant**: "how do I refund an order?" brings the right page and a link to it.

## Writing and editing

- **New page** (top of the list) starts a page. **Edit** on any page changes it.
- Pages are written in Markdown, with a live preview beside the text. The editor has buttons for the common things, and **Formatting help** at the bottom.
- Every save is kept in the page's **History**, with who changed it and when. Any earlier version can be restored, and a deleted page can be brought back from its history.
- Link to another page with `[[page-address]]`, and to an admin page with `[Text](admin:sales_order)`. See [[writing-wiki-pages]].

## Starter pages

The pages that came with the wiki are a starting point. Change them to match how this store works. A starter page nobody has edited is updated when the module is updated; once someone edits it, it is theirs and is left alone.

> [!TIP]
> House rules belong here too: who approves refunds, which carrier for which orders, what to check before a campaign goes out.

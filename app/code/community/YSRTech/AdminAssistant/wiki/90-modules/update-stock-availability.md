---
title: Update Stock Availability
category: This store's modules
sort: 960
---
When a product is saved with a stock quantity above 0, it is set **In Stock** automatically. It never sets a product out of stock.

It only acts when a product is saved with its stock (for example in [Manage Products](admin:catalog_product)). Stock changed by the POS sync does not trigger it.

See [[manage-stock]].

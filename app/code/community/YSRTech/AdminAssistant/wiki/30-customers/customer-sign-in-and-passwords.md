---
title: Customer sign-in and passwords
category: Customers
sort: 310
---
On this store customers can sign in with a one-time code emailed to them, instead of a password, when **Email OTP Login** is switched on. See [[otp-login]] for how it works and what to tell a customer who cannot get in.

When OTP login is off, the usual OpenMage sign-in applies:

- A customer who forgot their password uses **Forgot Your Password?** on the sign-in page.
- Staff can send a new password from the customer's **Account Information** tab: **Send Auto-Generated Password**, or type a **New Password**, then **Save Customer**.

If a customer gets no email at all (code or password), check that store email works: [[store-emails]].

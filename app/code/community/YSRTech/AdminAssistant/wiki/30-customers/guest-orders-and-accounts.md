---
title: Guest orders and customer accounts
category: Customers
sort: 330
---
A guest order (placed without signing in) is not attached to any account, so it does not show in **My Account** or on the customer's **Orders** tab.

On this store, the **Guest To Customer** module attaches guest orders to the account with the same email, and can create accounts for guests. See [[guest-to-customer]].

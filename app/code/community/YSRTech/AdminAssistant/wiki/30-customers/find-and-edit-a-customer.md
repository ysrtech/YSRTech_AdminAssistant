---
title: Find and edit a customer
category: Customers
sort: 300
---
1. [Customers > Manage Customers](admin:customer): filter by **Name**, **Email**, **Group**, **Customer Since**, then open the row.
2. Tabs on the customer:
   - **Customer View**: a summary, last orders and the shopping cart.
   - **Account Information**: name, email, group, date of birth.
   - **Addresses**: add, edit, choose the default billing and shipping address.
   - **Orders**, **Shopping Cart**, **Wishlist**, **Product Reviews**.
   - **Newsletter**: subscribed or not.
3. **Save Customer**.

A customer's email is their login. Changing it changes how they sign in.

> [!TIP]
> "Summarise this customer" in the AI Assistant, on the customer's page, gives their orders, lifetime value, group and newsletter status.

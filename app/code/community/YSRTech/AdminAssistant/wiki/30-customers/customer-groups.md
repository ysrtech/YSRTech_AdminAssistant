Groups sort customers for prices, discounts and taxes: for example retail, wholesale, staff.

- Manage them under [Customers > Customer Groups](admin:customer_group): **Add New Customer Group**, a name and a **Tax Class**.
- Move a customer: their **Account Information** tab > **Group** > **Save Customer**. Many at once: tick them on the customers grid, **Actions** > **Assign a Customer Group**.
- Use a group in: tier and group prices on products ([[prices-and-special-prices]]), cart and catalog price rules ([[coupons-and-cart-rules]]), Email Campaigns segments ([[email-campaigns-segments]]).

**NOT LOGGED IN** is the group of visitors who are not signed in. A price rule meant for everyone must include it.

New accounts made by [[guest-to-customer]] use the store's default group (**System > Configuration > Customer Configuration > Create New Account Options > Default Group**).

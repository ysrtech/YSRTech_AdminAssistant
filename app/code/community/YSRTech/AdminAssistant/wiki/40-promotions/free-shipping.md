---
title: Free shipping
category: Promotions
sort: 420
---
Two ways:

- **Above an order amount, for everyone**: [System > Configuration > Sales > Shipping Methods](admin:system_config/edit/section/carriers) > **Free Shipping**: **Enabled** = Yes and **Minimum Order Amount**. Customers above it see Free Shipping at checkout.
- **With conditions or a code**: a cart rule ([[coupons-and-cart-rules]]) with **Free Shipping** in its **Actions**. The **Free Shipping** method must be enabled as well.

See [[shipping-methods]].

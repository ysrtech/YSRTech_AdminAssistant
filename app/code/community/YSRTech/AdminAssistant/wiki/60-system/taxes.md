Taxes are made of four parts:

- **Tax zones and rates**: [Sales > Tax > Manage Tax Zones & Rates](admin:tax_rate): a rate per country/region (e.g. QC GST 5%, QC QST 9.975%).
- **Tax rules**: [Sales > Tax > Manage Tax Rules](admin:tax_rule): which customer tax classes and product tax classes get which rates, with a priority.
- **Classes**: products have a **Tax Class** (Prices tab); customer groups have a tax class.
- **Settings**: [System > Configuration > Sales > Tax](admin:system_config/edit/section/tax): whether prices include tax, how totals show.

> [!NOTE]
> On this store GST and QST show as separate lines, and QST is calculated on the running total ([[pardes-core]]). Change tax settings only with your accountant.

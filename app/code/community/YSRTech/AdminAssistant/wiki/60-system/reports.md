[Reports](admin:report_sales/sales): sales, tax, invoiced, shipping, refunds, coupons, products (best sellers, most viewed, low stock), customers, reviews, search terms.

> [!WARNING]
> Most sales reports read prepared statistics, not live orders. If a report looks empty or old, go to [Reports > Refresh Statistics](admin:report_statistics), tick the reports, and choose **Refresh Statistics for the Last Day** or **Refresh Lifetime Statistics**.

For quick answers ("sales this week compared with last week", "top 20 customers this year", "best sellers this month") ask the AI Assistant; it reads the live orders.

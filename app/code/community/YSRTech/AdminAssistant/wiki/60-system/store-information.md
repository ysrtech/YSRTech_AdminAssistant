---
title: Store name, address and contact details
category: Settings & system
sort: 600
---
- [System > Configuration > General](admin:system_config/edit/section/general): **Store Information** (name, phone, address shown on invoices and in emails), **Locale Options** (timezone, language, first day of week), **Countries Options**.
- **Store Email Addresses**: see [[store-emails]].
- **Web**: the store's addresses and the default pages (home page, 404 page).
- **Design**: logo, header and footer settings of the storefront theme.

Check the **Current Configuration Scope** at the top left ([[admin-basics]]).

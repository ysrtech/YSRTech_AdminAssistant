[System > Configuration > Sales > Shipping Methods](admin:system_config/edit/section/carriers)

- **Flat Rate**: one price **Per Order** or **Per Item**; **Title** and **Method Name** are what customers see; **Ship to Specific Countries** limits it.
- **Free Shipping**: **Minimum Order Amount** ([[free-shipping]]).
- **Table Rates**: prices by destination and weight, order total or item count. Switch the scope to the **website**, choose **Condition**, then **Export CSV**, fill in the spreadsheet, and **Import** it.
- Carriers (UPS, USPS, FedEx, DHL): need an account with the carrier.

**Shipping Settings > Origin** is the address shipments leave from (used for rates and taxes).

After a change, place a test order to check what customers see.

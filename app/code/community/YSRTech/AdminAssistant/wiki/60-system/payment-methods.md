[System > Configuration > Sales > Payment Methods](admin:system_config/edit/section/payment)

For each method: **Enabled**, **Title** (shown at checkout), **New Order Status**, **Payment from Applicable Countries**, **Minimum / Maximum Order Total**, **Sort Order**.

Offline methods (Check / Money Order, Bank Transfer, Cash On Delivery) only record the choice; staff confirm the payment and invoice the order ([[invoice-an-order]]).

> [!WARNING]
> Card gateway settings hold account keys. Change them only with the payment provider's instructions, and test with a small order after any change.

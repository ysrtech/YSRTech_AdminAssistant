## A new staff member

1. [System > Permissions > Users](admin:permissions_user) > **Add New User**.
2. **User Info**: **User Name**, names, **Email**, **Password**, **This account is** Active, and **Your Password** to confirm.
3. **User Role** tab: choose a role.
4. **Save User**.

## Roles

[System > Permissions > Roles](admin:permissions_role): a role is a set of permissions.

1. **Add New Role**, a **Role Name**, and **Your Password**.
2. **Role Resources**: **Resource Access** Custom, then tick what the role may open. A ticked parent gives its children unless they are unticked.
3. **Save Role**. Add users to it on the **Role Users** tab, or from each user's **User Role** tab.

On this store, look for:

- **AI Assistant (use the chat)** and, under it, **What the assistant may look up** (orders, customers, catalogue, sales, Email Campaigns results, store settings and logs, report query, how-to help). See [[ai-assistant-permissions]].
- **Wiki (read)** and **Write, edit and delete pages**.
- **Email Campaigns** and each of its pages.
- **System > AI Assistant Audit Log**.

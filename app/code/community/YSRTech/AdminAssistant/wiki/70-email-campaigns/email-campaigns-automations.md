An automation sends emails by itself when something happens. [Email Campaigns > Automations](admin:ysrtech_automation) > **New Flow**.

Triggers:

- **Order placed**, **Order reached a status**, **Order shipped**, **Invoice raised**, **Refunded**
- **Bought a particular product**
- **Left a cart behind** (after **Abandoned Cart After (hours)** in the Sending settings)

The flow is drawn as steps: each step waits a set time, then sends an email (from a template). A flow can stop when the person places an order. Switch it **Active** to start it; the cards show what each flow has sent and earned.

Editing an active flow makes a new version; people already part-way through finish the version they started.

Results per automation and per step: on its page, or ask the AI Assistant "how is the order follow-up doing?".

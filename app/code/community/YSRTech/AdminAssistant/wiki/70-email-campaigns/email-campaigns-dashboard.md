[Email Campaigns > Dashboard](admin:ysrtech_dashboard), over 7, 14 or 30 days against the period before:

- **The verdict** at the top: a one-line judgement of sender reputation.
- **Sent / Delivered**: delivered is sent minus bounced.
- **Real opens**: opens by people, not counting the automatic opens made by mail scanners (shown separately). Opens are always undercounted a little: some mail apps block the tracking image.
- **Clicks**, **Bounce rate**, **Spam rate**.
- **By mailbox provider**: Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo… A problem at one provider shows here first.

Rough guides: bounce rate under 2% and spam rate under 0.1% are healthy; above those, check the list's quality and the [[email-campaigns-suppressions]].

**Orders and revenue** credited to an email: an order placed within the credit window after a click on that email ([[email-campaigns-sending-setup]], Tracking).

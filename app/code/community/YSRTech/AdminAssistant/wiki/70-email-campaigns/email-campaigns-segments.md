A segment is a group of contacts chosen by rules, kept up to date as contacts change.

[Email Campaigns > Segments](admin:ysrtech_segment): create one and add rules, combined with **all** or **any**. Rules can use:

- **Email Address**, **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Store View**
- **Has A Customer Account**, **Customer Group**, **Days Since Account Created**
- **Number Of Orders**, **Total Spent**, **Average Order Value**, **Days Since Last Order**

Examples:

- Repeat customers: Number Of Orders is 2 or more.
- Lapsed: Days Since Last Order is more than 180.
- Wholesale: Customer Group is Wholesale.

The segment page shows how many people it holds now. A campaign can send to several segments and leave others out ([[email-campaigns-send-a-campaign]]).

Suppressed addresses are never mailed, whatever segment they are in ([[email-campaigns-suppressions]]).

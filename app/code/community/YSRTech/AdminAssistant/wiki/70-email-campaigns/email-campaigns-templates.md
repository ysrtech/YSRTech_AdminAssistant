[Email Campaigns > Templates](admin:ysrtech_template) > **New Template**:

- **Use This** on a starter design from the gallery, **Start Blank**, or **Start From My Own HTML**.
- **Design With AI** writes a first draft from a short brief (only when AI is switched on in the settings; it sends the brief and the store's name, never customer data).

The designer is drag and drop: blocks on the side (text, image, button, products, coupon, divider…) go into the email; click a block to change it. Reusable **sections** can be saved and used again. Placeholders such as the subscriber's first name are filled in for each recipient, with a fallback for people with no name on file.

## The header and footer

The store's logo, colours, fonts, header links and footer come from [System > Configuration > YSRTech > Email Campaigns > Brand](admin:system_config/edit/section/ysrtech_emailcampaigns). The footer always carries the store's name, its postal address (from **General > Store Information**) and the unsubscribe link.

> [!NOTE]
> On this store, two starter templates, **Weekly New Releases** and **Weekly New Releases + Shop by Category**, are in the gallery under **Pardes newsletters**, with Pardes's colours and fonts as the brand defaults ([[pardes-core]]).

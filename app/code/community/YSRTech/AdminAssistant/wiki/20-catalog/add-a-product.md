title: Add a product
category: Catalog
sort: 200
---
1. Go to [Catalog > Manage Products](admin:catalog_product) and click **Add Product**.
2. Choose the **Attribute Set** (the set of fields) and **Product Type** (**Simple Product** for a single item), then **Continue**.
3. **General** tab: **Name**, **Description**, **Short Description**, **SKU** (must be unique), **Weight**, **Status** (Enabled), **Visibility** (Catalog, Search), **URL Key** (leave empty to make it from the name).
4. **Prices** tab: **Price** and **Tax Class**. See [[prices-and-special-prices]].
5. **Images** tab: **Browse Files**, **Upload Files**, then pick the **Base Image**, **Small Image** and **Thumbnail** radio buttons for the main picture.
6. **Inventory** tab: **Qty** and **Stock Availability** (In Stock). See [[manage-stock]].
7. **Websites** tab: tick the website.
8. **Categories** tab: tick the categories.
9. Click **Save** (or **Save and Continue Edit**).

A product only shows on the storefront when it is **Enabled**, **In Stock** (unless out-of-stock products are shown), visible, assigned to the website, and the indexes are up to date ([[cache-and-indexes]]).

> [!TIP]
> To make a similar product faster, open an existing one and click **Duplicate**. Change the SKU, name and URL key, then enable it.

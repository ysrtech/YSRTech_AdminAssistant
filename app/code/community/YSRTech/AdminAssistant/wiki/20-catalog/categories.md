[Catalog > Manage Categories](admin:catalog_category)

- **Add a category**: select its parent in the tree, then **Add Subcategory**. Fill **Name**, set **Is Active** = Yes, **Include in Navigation Menu** as wanted, and **Save Category**.
- **Order in the menu**: drag categories in the tree.
- **Products in it**: the **Category Products** tab; tick products (filter **Any** to see all) and save. Or tick categories on each product's **Categories** tab.
- **Display Settings** tab: **Display Mode** (products, a static block, or both), **Is Anchor** (shows products of subcategories too, and enables layered navigation), **Default Product Listing Sort By**.

> [!NOTE]
> On this store, listings sort **Newest First** by default ([[pardes-core]]). A category can choose a different sort in its Display Settings.

The category's ID shows in the heading of its edit page. The [[showcase-widgets]] use category IDs.

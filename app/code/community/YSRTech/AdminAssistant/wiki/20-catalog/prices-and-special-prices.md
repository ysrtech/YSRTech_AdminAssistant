---
title: Prices, sale prices and tier prices
category: Catalog
sort: 230
---
On the product's **Prices** tab:

- **Price**: the regular price.
- **Special Price** with **Special Price From Date** / **To Date**: a sale price for a period. Leave the dates empty for "until removed". The storefront shows the regular price crossed out.
- **Tier Price**: cheaper per unit from a quantity (e.g. 3 or more at 9.99), per website and customer group.
- **Group Price**: a different price for a customer group.

To discount many products by a rule (a category, a brand, a date range), use a catalog price rule instead: [[catalog-price-rules]]. For a discount at checkout or with a code: [[coupons-and-cart-rules]].

Many products at once: **Update Attributes** in the products grid's **Actions** can set **Special Price** and dates for the ticked products.
